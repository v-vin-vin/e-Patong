♻️ E-Waste Information

Learn about e-waste categories, their hazards, and how to dispose of them properly in the Philippines.

⚠️ Philippine E-Waste Crisis

The Philippines generated approximately 537,000 metric tons of e-waste in 2022 (4.7 kg per capita), yet the majority is handled by the informal sector using hazardous methods. Improper disposal releases lead, mercury, and cadmium into soil and water sources. Under RA 9003 and the EPR Act of 2022, producers and consumers share responsibility for proper e-waste management.

E-Waste Categories

📱
Mobile Phone

Smartphones and feature phones contain recoverable gold, silver, copper, and platinum in their circuit boards.

💰 Scrap value: ₱50 – ₱200  |  ⭐ 50 pts
LeadMercuryCadmium

Remove battery first. Wipe all personal data before drop-off.

💻
Laptop / Notebook

Laptops contain valuable aluminum casings, copper wiring, and recoverable rare earth metals in their components.

💰 Scrap value: ₱200 – ₱800  |  ⭐ 150 pts
LeadCadmiumChromium

Backup and wipe your hard drive. Remove and separately recycle the battery.

📱
Tablet

Tablets share similar composition to smartphones with lithium batteries and printed circuit boards requiring careful recycling.

💰 Scrap value: ₱100 – ₱400  |  ⭐ 80 pts
LithiumLead

Remove SIM and memory cards. Factory reset before drop-off.

🔋
Battery

Lithium-ion and lead-acid batteries are highly hazardous if improperly discarded and must never be thrown in regular trash.

💰 Scrap value: ₱15 – ₱50  |  ⭐ 30 pts
LithiumLeadSulfuric Acid

Never puncture, crush, or incinerate. Drop at designated battery collection points.

🔌
Charger / Adapter

Chargers contain copper wiring and circuit components that are recyclable. Cables are made from PVC plastic requiring special processing.

💰 Scrap value: ₱10 – ₱40  |  ⭐ 20 pts
PVCLead

Bundle cables neatly. Drop at any e-waste bin or participating electronics store.

🖥️
Circuit Board (PCB)

Printed circuit boards contain the highest concentration of precious metals including gold, silver, and palladium — the most valuable e-waste type.

💰 Scrap value: ₱100 – ₱400  |  ⭐ 100 pts
LeadArsenicBeryllium

Place in anti-static bag. Wear gloves — components may have sharp edges and toxic residue.

🖥️
Monitor / Display

LCD monitors contain mercury in backlight lamps. CRT monitors have lead-containing glass requiring specialized recycling.

💰 Scrap value: ₱150 – ₱500  |  ⭐ 120 pts
LeadMercuryCadmium

Do not crack the screen. CRTs are especially hazardous — handle carefully and transport upright.

⌨️
Keyboard

Keyboards are primarily plastic and metal. They contain small circuit boards and may have brominated flame retardants in their casings.

💰 Scrap value: ₱20 – ₱80  |  ⭐ 25 pts
PVCBFRs

Can be disassembled for separate plastic and metal recovery at most recycling facilities.

🖱️
Mouse

Computer mice contain small circuit boards, optical sensors, and copper wiring within plastic enclosures.

💰 Scrap value: ₱10 – ₱40  |  ⭐ 15 pts
PVCBFRs

Remove batteries if any. Drop at e-waste collection points or electronics stores.

📺
Television

TVs are large, bulky e-waste. Older CRT televisions contain several kg of lead in their glass tubes.

💰 Scrap value: ₱200 – ₱600  |  ⭐ 130 pts
LeadMercuryCadmium

Contact the Barangay Hall of Brgy. 49 Youngfield for scheduled e-waste collection days for bulky items.

🏛️ Philippine Legal Framework

  • RA 9003 — Ecological Solid Waste Management Act: Mandates proper segregation and disposal of all solid waste including electronics.
  • RA 6969 — Toxic Substances and Hazardous Waste Act: Regulates importation, manufacture, and disposal of hazardous materials found in e-waste.
  • EPR Act of 2022 — Extended Producer Responsibility Act: Requires brand owners and importers to establish e-waste take-back programs.

⚡ Setting Up Your AI Classification Model

e-Patong uses a Google Teachable Machine model for image classification. Follow these steps to train and add your model:

  1. Go to teachablemachine.withgoogle.com and create a new Image Project.
  2. Create 10 classes — one for each e-waste category (Mobile Phone, Laptop, Tablet, Battery, Charger/Adapter, Circuit Board, Monitor/Display, Keyboard, Mouse, Television). Class names must match exactly.
  3. Add at least 30–50 photos per class. Use varied angles, lighting, and backgrounds.
  4. Click Train Model and wait for training to complete.
  5. Click Export Model → TensorFlow.js → Download. You'll get a ZIP file.
  6. Extract the ZIP and copy model.json, metadata.json, and weights.bin into frontend/assets/model/.
  7. Restart the server and reload the Scan page — the model will load automatically.
