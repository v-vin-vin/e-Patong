How e-Patong Works

Three simple steps to responsible e-waste disposal.

1

Scan Your E-Waste

Upload a photo or use your camera to capture your electronic item. No account needed for this step.

2

Get Classification & Value

Our AI classifies the item, checks its condition, shows the scrap value, and guides you on proper disposal.

3

Earn Points & Redeem

Log in with Google to save scans and earn points. Exchange your points for GCash credits, e-load, and more.
