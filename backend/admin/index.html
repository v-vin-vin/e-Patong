Confidence levels

The AI gives a confidence number for its best guess. These two cutoffs decide what happens to a scan. The server uses them for every scan.

Below this → ❌ LOW. The item is not identified and earns no points.
At or above this → ✅ HIGH (normal result). Between the two → ⚠️ MEDIUM (the user picks a guess).

Accuracy

What users tell us: "Was this right?" on HIGH results, and which guess they pick on MEDIUM results. These are the users' own answers, not a lab test. Few answers means an unreliable number.
